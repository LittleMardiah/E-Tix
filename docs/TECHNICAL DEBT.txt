### Ide #1: README.md dengan Storytelling

**Deskripsi:**
README.md di repo GitHub tidak boleh hanya berisi daftar fitur teknis. Harus ada **cerita** yang membuat recruiter/developer tertarik.

**Isi yang Disarankan:**

```markdown
# E-Tix Flash - Smart Ticketing Platform

## The Story Behind This Project

> "I was at a concert with 3 friends. We booked tickets together, but at checkout, 1 seat was gone. We ended up sitting separately. It ruined the experience."

This project was born from that frustration.

## Why I Built This

I wanted to solve 3 real problems:
1. **Server crashes** during flash sales → 20.000 RPS design.
2. **Scalpers/bots** stealing tickets → 95% bot blocking.
3. **Groups can't sit together** → Atomic group locking.

## What Makes This Different

- **Anti-Bot Ingress:** 2-stage pipeline (synchronous + asynchronous).
- **Bitmask Locking:** Redis Lua script for atomic group locking.
- **Split-Pay + Bulk:** Flexible payment for groups.
- **Dynamic QR + Fallback PIN:** Security + user convenience.
- **Controlled Resale:** Price cap +10%.
- **Admin Emergency Tools:** Bulk cancellation & refund.

## Tech Stack

- **Backend:** Golang 1.22+ (Gin, pgx, Redis)
- **Database:** PostgreSQL 15+ (Supabase)
- **Cache:** Redis Cluster (Bitmask Locking, Rate Limiting)
- **Message Broker:** RabbitMQ (Delayed Queue, Fan Score)
- **Deployment:** Render.com + Docker

## Live Demo

[Link to demo](https://e-tix-flash-demo.vercel.app)

**Try it yourself:**
- Login: `customer@demo.com` / `password123`
- Select 4 seats, experience the 3-minute timer!

## What I Learned

1. **Concurrency is hard:** Pessimistic locking, atomic operations, race conditions.
2. **Users are unpredictable:** That's why I added Static Fallback PIN and Bulk Payment.
3. **Production is different:** Monitoring, alerting, disaster recovery matter.

## Competitive Analysis - E-Tix Flash vs Competitors

| Feature | Loket | GoShow | Tiket.com | Eventbrite | E-Tix Flash |
|---------|-------|--------|-----------|------------|-------------|

| Atomic Group Locking | ❌ | ❌ | ❌ | ❌ | ✅ |

## Mengapa EO akan pindah ke E-Tix Flash?

1. **Masalah #1: Server Crash** → E-Tix Flash handle 20.000 RPS dengan Redis Bitmask Lock.
2. **Masalah #2: Calo Borong** → E-Tix Flash blokir 95% bot di pintu masuk.
3. **Masalah #3: Fans Kecewa** → E-Tix Flash jamin kursi grup dan kasih fallback PIN.
4. **Masalah #4: Calo Jual Mahal** → E-Tix Flash kendalikan harga resale max +10%.
5. **Masalah #5: Admin Panik** → E-Tix Flash kasih Emergency Tools untuk bulk cancel & refund.

## Target Pasar Awal

- **Event Organizer Menengah-Besar** (5.000-50.000 penonton) yang sudah muak dengan calo dan server crash.
- **Brand / Sponsor** yang peduli dengan citra dan keadilan.

Ide #4: Business Model & Pricing Strategy
Deskripsi:

Isi yang Disarankan:

markdown
## 💰 BUSINESS MODEL & PRICING STRATEGY

### Revenue Streams (V1.0)

| Revenue Stream | Deskripsi | Estimasi |
|----------------|-----------|----------|
| **Platform Fee (Primary)** | Fee per tiket terjual (2.5% dari harga tiket) | Jika 10.000 tiket × Rp 1.000.000 × 2.5% = Rp 250.000.000 per event |
| **Resale Fee (Secondary)** | Fee 1% dari transaksi resale (untuk operasional) | Jika 10% tiket di-resale → tambahan Rp 25.000.000 per event |

### Pricing Tier untuk EO

| Tier | Harga | Fitur |
|------|-------|-------|
| **Free** | 0% fee | Untuk event < 500 tiket (limited features) |
| **Pro** | 2.5% fee | Full features, 20.000 RPS, priority support |
| **Enterprise** | Custom | Dedicated infra, white-label, custom integration |

### Keunggulan Kompetitif Harga

- Loket/GoShow/Tiket.com: 5-10% fee.
- E-Tix Flash: 2.5% fee (lebih murah 50-75%).
- Dengan harga lebih murah, EO bisa jual tiket lebih murah ke fans ATAU dapat profit lebih besar.

### Break-Even Analysis (Estimasi)

| Event Size | Fee Income | Infra Cost (Render + Supabase) | Profit |
|------------|------------|--------------------------------|--------|
| 1.000 tiket × Rp 500.000 × 2.5% | Rp 12.500.000 | Rp 500.000 | Rp 12.000.000 |
| 10.000 tiket × Rp 1.000.000 × 2.5% | Rp 250.000.000 | Rp 1.000.000 | Rp 249.000.000 |
Alasan Penting:

Menunjukkan product thinking: Recruiter suka kandidat yang paham "software adalah bisnis".

Membuktikan kelayakan produk: Produk hebat tapi gak menghasilkan uang = sia-sia.

Rare skill: Hanya sedikit engineer yang punya analisis bisnis di portofolio.
